# Hara Desktop 1.1.0

- A new workspace layout puts Hara, colleagues, private chats and tasks within easy reach. Light and dark themes fill the window with an oxblood accent.
- Create personal channels on this Mac and add colleagues from the current workspace, without a cloud login or a channel member quota. Ask everyone or selected colleagues to reply, or save a note without requesting a reply. Channel history is saved locally in the current workspace.
- Find channel members and reply recipients by name or role. Search keeps your selections, and the reply summary shows how many colleagues will respond. Channel controls fit smaller chat areas, and save actions remain accessible in shorter windows.
- Open local web previews beside the conversation. Expand, restore or close the right panel while keeping your chat and draft available.
- The interface follows your system language by default. Choose English, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese in Settings. Consecutive operations share a compact row with expandable details.
- Switches now keep their capsule shape without a rectangular background, in both themes and when hovered, focused or disabled.
- Settings now displays “1 connection” for a single active connection and the plural form for zero or multiple connections.

Inviting other people is a placeholder in this version; no launch date is promised. Personal channels work with AI colleagues in your local workspace.
